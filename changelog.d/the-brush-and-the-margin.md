---
title: The Brush That Turned and the Margin That Didn't
kind: fix
try: On an iPad held upright, brush a finger a short way across the page, then tap right at its left or right edge.
---
On the big iPad, the page had its moods backwards. A finger that barely brushed it flipped a whole page, and a firm tap at the very edge of the screen, out in the margin, fell into nothing at all. Readers lost their place to a brush, and the margin, where a thumb reaches first, would not take them back. Now a brush shorter than a fingertip leaves the page where it is, and a tap in the margin beside the text turns it, just as a tap at the page's edge always did.
