---
title: The Cloud That Changed Its Locks
kind: fix
try: Tap a book that's only in your cloud, then open one you were reading on your e-reader.
---
Overnight the cloud rearranged its rooms. It began labelling every book with a fingerprint no file could match, so each download was turned away at the door as a forgery. It started keeping the e-reader's place in a back drawer you had to ask for by name, so a book you'd read halfway on the e-reader opened at its first page. And it took down the sign the app read to sign in at all. Now books come down whole and are checked by their size when the fingerprint is false, the app asks for the drawer, and sign-in knows the way in. A sign-in the cloud fumbles is asked again before you're told your password is wrong.
