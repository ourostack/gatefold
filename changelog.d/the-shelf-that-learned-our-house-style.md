title: The Shelf That Learned Our House Style
kind: polish
try: Turn on Airplane Mode with an empty shelf, then open Fine-Tune in any book.
---
An empty shelf, a lost connection and a cloud in a sulk each used to speak in their own home-made voice.
Now they speak Ouro's: the empty and unreachable shelves are one calm empty state with proper buttons, the
cloud's trouble arrives as a warning banner with Try Again inside it, and Fine-Tune's rows wear matching icon
tiles. Same words, same buttons, one family.
