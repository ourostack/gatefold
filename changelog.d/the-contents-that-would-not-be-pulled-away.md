---
title: The Contents That Would Not Be Pulled Away
kind: fix
try: On the folded Duo, open Contents, scroll it, then pull it down by its top without choosing a chapter.
---
On the cover screen, Contents opens on his chapter, part way down a long list. Its top was a bar the list slid under, and pulling the sheet down from that bar only scrolled the list, so Contents stayed up. The next tap meant for the page landed on a chapter instead, and his place jumped twice in one review. The tabs and Close now sit in a header that nothing scrolls under, and a pull that starts there takes Contents away and leaves his page as it was.
