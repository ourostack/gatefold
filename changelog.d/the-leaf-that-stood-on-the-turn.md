---
title: The Leaf That Stood Still on the Turn
kind: fix
try: On an iPad, open a book, jump to a chapter from Contents, read two pages, then turn the iPad on its side and back upright.
---
Turned on its side for the first time, the iPad left the old page up with the right page blank, and the leaf rising from the spine stood frozen over it for six seconds or more. Turned back upright, the chapter's title page flashed past and he landed a page further on than he had been. Now the spread lays out without loading the chapter again, so the leaf swings open while it is still rising, and the page keeps the margins it was read in, so turning back puts him on his own page.
