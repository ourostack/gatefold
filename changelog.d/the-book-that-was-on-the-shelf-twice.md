---
title: The Book That Was on the Shelf Twice
kind: fix
try: Sign out, tap Try a Few Classics, read a page of one, then sign in to an account that has the same book.
---
A book you added before signing in, from Files or the classics on the empty shelf, no longer shows up a second time when your PocketBook account has the same file. It is one book on the shelf, linked to the Cloud so its place goes to and from your e-reader, and it keeps where you were, your bookmarks, its cover and your highlights.
