---
title: The Jump That Was Saved Too Late
try: In Pride and Prejudice, drag the scrubber far into the book (or open a bookmark, highlight or search result in a far chapter), go Home before the chapter appears, swipe Gatefold away, then open it again.
---
He leapt deep into the book, by the scrubber, a bookmark, a highlight or a search result, and while the far chapter was still loading, went Home. Gatefold meant to save where he had gone, but first it waited on the loading page, and the phone ended the app before that wait was over. The book reopened at its very start. Now the place any jump sends him is saved the moment he leaves, before any wait, and the book reopens where he went.
