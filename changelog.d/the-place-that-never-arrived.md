---
title: The Place That Never Arrived
kind: fix
try: Read a few pages of a cloud book, close it, and look under the Library title.
---
A reader turned twenty pages on the phone and closed the book, and the shelf promised it was in step with the e-reader. Behind the scenes the cloud had refused the place and the app had quietly thrown it away, so the e-reader woke up twenty pages behind. Now a place the cloud turns away is never lost in silence: the shelf says plainly that places read here aren't reaching the e-reader, and it says it again after a relaunch, until one gets through.
