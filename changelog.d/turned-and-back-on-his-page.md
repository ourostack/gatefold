title: Turned And Back, On His Page
try: On an iPad, jump to a chapter from Contents, turn the iPad on its side, then turn it back upright.
---
After the iPad turned, the reader put his words back on screen, but the book could still lay the page out once more a moment later and land a page before or after his. Nothing moved it back, and sometimes that other page was even taken as his place. Now, when the page moves after a turn without him touching it, his words are found and put back, on one page or on a spread.
