---
title: A Folder of One's Own
kind: feature
try: In the Add menu, choose Watch a Folder and pick a folder of EPUBs in iCloud Drive. Put another book in it from a Mac, then come back to Gatefold.
---
Every reader deserves a folder of their own, and now Gatefold will keep one for you. Pick a folder in iCloud Drive, or anywhere Files can reach, and every EPUB inside walks onto your shelf. Drop more books in later, from your Mac or anywhere else, and they join the shelf the next time you come back to Gatefold. Pick the same folder on your iPad too, and the two keep each other on the same page: the place you read to last travels through the folder itself, with no account and no PocketBook needed. Stop watching whenever you like; your books stay put.
