---
title: The Place That Left No Footprints
kind: polish
try: Read a few pages, lock the phone, then open the same book on your e-reader.
---
A reader closed Gatefold mid-chapter, picked up the e-reader, and found it stubbornly on an older page. Did the place leave the phone? Did the Cloud turn it away? Nobody could say, because it left no footprints. Now every send and fetch of the reading place leaves a small, nameless note: whether it went, waited or was turned away, and why. No titles, no pages and no words from the book are ever in it.
