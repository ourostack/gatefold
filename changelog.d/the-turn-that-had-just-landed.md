---
title: The Turn That Had Just Landed
try: On an iPad, tap to turn the page and turn the iPad the moment the page lands.
---
A reader on an iPad turned the page, and turned the iPad just as the page landed. The landing had not yet said where he was, and the busy reader put him back on the page he had turned from. Now the words of the page he turned to are his place the moment it lands, so the rotation keeps it.
