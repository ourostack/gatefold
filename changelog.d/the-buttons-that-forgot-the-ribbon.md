title: The Buttons That Forgot the Ribbon
kind: polish
try: Open Gatefold with an empty library and look at Add Books from Files.
---
When the shelves learned our house style, their buttons came home dressed in a stranger's blue. Add Books from Files on an empty shelf wore it, and so did Try Again when the shelf could not be reached. They stood out like a borrowed coat at a wedding.
They wear the ribbon now, deep blue by day and pale blue by night, the same silk as the bookmark.
