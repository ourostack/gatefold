---
title: The Page That Slid Off the Edge
try: In scroll mode on the folded Duo, drag the page sideways and let go.
---
In scroll mode, a stray sideways drag could pull the whole page across the screen, and the start of every line went over the edge. Readers tilted their heads. Some scrolled down, hoping the next paragraph would come back. It didn't. A scrolling page now moves only up and down, and every line stays where it starts.
