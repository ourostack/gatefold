The e-reader's place arrived, and the page lifted its words to the top of the left page, right where they belonged. But on a busy iPad its memory looked a heartbeat too early, saw the page as it was before that move, and kept that one. The screen was right, but the reopen came back one spread early. Now the page checks that the words it keeps are the ones it just put on top, and looks again until they are.
