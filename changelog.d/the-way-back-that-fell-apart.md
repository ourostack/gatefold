A reader on the folded Duo leapt across the book with the scrubber, reached for "Back to where you were", and found it crushed into a tower of letter fragments, leaning on the scrubber while the whole bar lurched up over the text. Now the pill keeps its words on one line. Where the bar has no room beside the scrubber, it stands just above it, as it does with the controls down, and the bar stays exactly where it was.
