---
title: The Turn The iPad Swallowed
try: On an iPad, tap to turn the page and turn the iPad on its side at once, then turn it back upright.
---
A reader tapped to turn the page and, in the same breath, turned his iPad on its side. The page turned, and then it was gone. Back upright he was on the page he had just left, or a page further back, once even in the chapter before, and the next time he opened the book it kept that wrong page. The turn had not finished landing when the new layout began, so the reader put back the page from before it. Now the page he turns to is read the moment the tap reaches it, and that is the page the iPad brings back.
