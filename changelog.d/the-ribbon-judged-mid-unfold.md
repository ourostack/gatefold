---
title: The Spread Judged Before It Was Drawn
try: Bookmark a page on the Duo, fold it half open and then flat again, and look for the ribbon.
---
Half open, then flat again: the Duo swings the spread wide and puts the reader's sentence back at the top of the left page. But the app could look at the spread a heartbeat too soon, while it was still being drawn, and judge the page by a place it had already left, the bookmark ribbon included. Now, once the sentence is back in place, it reads the spread from the page itself, and looks again until the page shows its words.
