---
title: The Spread Judged Before It Was Drawn
try: Bookmark a page on the Duo, fold it half open and then flat again, and look for the ribbon.
---
Half open, then flat again: the Duo swings the spread wide and puts the reader's sentence back at the top of the left page. But the app looked at the spread a heartbeat too soon, while it was still being drawn, found no words at all, and judged the page by a place it had already left. The bookmark ribbon was decided on that empty look. Now it waits for the spread to show its words before it decides anything.
