---
title: Seven Classics in the Hold
kind: feature
try: Sign out, or install fresh and tap Read Without an Account, then Try a Few Classics.
---
A reader with no PocketBook account came aboard and found the shelves bare: no book to open, nothing to turn. Gatefold had seven classics stowed below deck all along, from Frankenstein to Emma, and never offered one. Now an empty shelf offers them. One tap brings them up, ready to read with no account and no download.
