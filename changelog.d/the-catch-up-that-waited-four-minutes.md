---
title: The Catch-Up That Waited Four Minutes
try: On a busy iPad, open a book whose other device is further on and tap Catch up the moment it slides out.
---
Catch up loads his other device's page behind the one he is reading, so the tap only has to swap it in. If that page was slow, the tap was meant to give up on it after a second and a half and jump the ordinary way. It never gave up. On a struggling iPad one tap waited four minutes for the page behind, while the veil let go and showed a page with nothing on it. Now the tap waits its second and a half, then jumps, and the page comes.
