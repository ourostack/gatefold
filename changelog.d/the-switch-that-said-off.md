---
title: The Switch That Said Off
try: Open Your Page, then Fine-Tune, and turn Justify off and on.
---
Every page stood in neat, justified columns, and yet Fine-Tune swore Justify was Off. Testers flipped it. Nothing happened. They flipped it back. Still nothing. The switch now tells the truth: it starts On, because the page is justified, and Off sets the lines ragged, as it says. If you had it Off before, it reads On now, so your page looks just as it did.
