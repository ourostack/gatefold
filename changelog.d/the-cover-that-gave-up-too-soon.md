---
title: The Cover That Gave Up Too Soon
try: Fold the Duo shut from an open spread while the device is busy, and read the cover.
---
Midway through Pride and Prejudice, a reader folded the Duo shut, and the cover went looking for his sentence. It sent the page back once, glanced for a split second, saw the wrong words, and sent it again, three times in a breath, then gave up and called the place lost. The page was only slow: it arrived a moment later, sentence and all. Now the cover watches a slow page land before sending it again, so the fold keeps his place even when the device is struggling.
