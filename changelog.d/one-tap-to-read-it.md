---
title: One Tap, and the Book Comes Home
kind: feature
try: Tap a book that says In Cloud, once, and wait on the shelf. Then tap another and leave the app while it downloads.
---
A reader tapped a book still in the Cloud, watched it arrive, and then had to tap it all over again, as if the shelf had already forgotten what he asked for. Now one tap downloads the book and opens it, with a bar beneath its cover showing how far it has got. Go somewhere else while it downloads, and it waits on the shelf marked Ready. Changed your mind? Tap it again, or press and hold and choose Stop Download, and it goes back to the Cloud.
