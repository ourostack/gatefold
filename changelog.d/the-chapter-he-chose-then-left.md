title: The Chapter He Chose, Then Left Before It Loaded
try: In Pride and Prejudice, open Contents, tap a chapter deep in the book, leave at once and reopen it.

He tapped a chapter in Contents and left before it had finished loading. The jump was thrown away, and the book came back on the page he had been reading before he chose it. Now the chapter he chose is kept as his place even while it is still on its way, and the reopen goes there. If he only stepped away, the jump carries on and keeps the page it lands on.
