---
title: The Page That Turned With the iPad
kind: polish
try: Open a book on an iPad, turn it on its side, then back upright, and watch the page between.
---
Turn the iPad on its side and, for a heartbeat, the page broke: cut off at the edge, a grey strip standing over it, and on the way back upright a squeezed spread sliding out of view. No more. The page you were reading stays whole, at its own shape, until the new layout is ready, then the new pages fade in over it.
