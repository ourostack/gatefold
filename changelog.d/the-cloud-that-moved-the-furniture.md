---
title: The Cloud That Moved the Furniture
kind: fix
try: Sign in, open your library, and open a book you were reading on your e-reader.
---
The cloud has a habit of rearranging itself without warning. Now, when it moves something the app can do without, such as the label on your account or the time beside a place, the app steps around it instead of tripping. You stay signed in, the shelf still fills, and the book still opens where the e-reader left it. Each thing it stepped around is written down, so the next change is fixed before it trips anyone.
