A reader on a small screen searched for a name, tapped the result, and the book obligingly went there, underlined the very word, and then parked the "Back to where you were" button right on top of it, leaving only the last two letters peeking out. Now the controls step aside with the search sheet, and if the word sits low on the page, the way back waits at the top instead.
