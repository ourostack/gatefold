---
title: The Number That Vanished on Its Side
try: Open a long book with the iPad on its side, and watch the page number while it settles.
---
Turn the iPad on its side, open a long book, and wait. For a minute the page was there and the number was coming, until the app lost patience, decided the count had died, and left an empty slot where the number should be.
The count was never dead, only held while the page finished turning to fit the screen. Now the app knows the difference: a held count keeps its place, and the number arrives.
