---
title: The Second Factor at the Gate
try: Sign in with an account whose store supports a password, then browse the shelf and open a book.
---
A correct password could be turned away twice by the Cloud, leaving the shelf, its downloads and the reader's place beyond the gate. Gatefold gives a third try before deciding the password was wrong. It also recognizes stores that demand a second factor and does not offer a password sign-in it cannot complete.
