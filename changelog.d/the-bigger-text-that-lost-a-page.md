---
title: The Bigger Text That Lost a Page
kind: fix
try: On a Duo, with a few other apps busy, open a book, turn a page, then make the text larger in Your Page. Check the page still holds the words you were reading.
---
Make the text bigger on a busy device, and the book has to lay itself out all over again while you wait. Sometimes it was still working when the reader went looking for your page, and it gave up and left you one page early, your words just out of sight. Now the reader waits for the pages to stop moving, finds your words wherever they ended up, and takes you right back to them.
