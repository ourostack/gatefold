---
title: The Book That Tried to Open as It Closed
try: On the iPhone Duo, stand Pride and Prejudice half open as a tabletop, then fold it shut and read the cover screen.
---
He stood the Duo half open on the table, then folded it shut. For one confused second the book tried to open on the inner screen he had just closed, laying out two facing pages nobody could see. By the time the cover screen took over, the page had raced past his place, sometimes several pages on. Now a closed inner screen leaves the page alone until the cover screen takes over, so folding shut from tabletop opens to the page he was reading.
