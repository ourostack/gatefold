---
title: The Page That Ghosted Through Itself
try: On an iPad with two pages open, turn it on its side and back, and watch the moment the new layout appears.
---
Turn the iPad and the page you were reading would hang there for most of a second after the new one was already laid out underneath. Then the two faded into each other, the new lines showing through the old like a double exposure, before the old page gave up. Now the old page leaves the moment the new one has taken its size, and the new layout arrives in a single frame, whole, with no ghost.
