---
title: The Bar That Stepped Up
try: Open a book, tap the middle of the page, and tap Contents as soon as the bar appears.
---
The first time the controls came up after opening a book, their bottom bar landed a little too low and then stepped up into place. A quick tap on Contents could land just under where it had settled, on the page itself, and the controls went away instead of Contents opening. The bar now lands where it belongs from its first moment, so the tap opens Contents.
