title: The Reopen That Lifted Its Cover Too Soon

He had chosen his chapter, and the book had kept it. But on a slow reopen the cover grew tired of waiting and lifted early, showing the start of the chapter's file instead, with his place offered only as a banner. Now the cover waits a little longer, until his own page is ready, and then lifts on it. If the page takes far too long, the banner still offers the way back.
