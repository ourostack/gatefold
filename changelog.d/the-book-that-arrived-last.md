---
title: The Book That Arrived Last
try: With several books still downloading, open the app straight into one of them and wait for it to land.
---
He asked for one book as the app woke. The shelf filled, book after book, and his landed last of all. The app was busy counting the shelf, lost count on that final arrival, and his book stayed shut while he watched the others settle. Now the library calls out each book as it lands, and the one he asked for opens the moment it is here, first or last.
