---
title: The Switch That Cut the Book Into New Pages
try: In Pride and Prejudice, open Your Page, change the paper, then in Fine-Tune flip Justify on and off.
---
A third of the way into Pride and Prejudice, a reader flipped Justify and the page jumped back, his sentence now halfway down a page he had already read. On the folded Duo, even a new paper colour did it. Both had quietly changed how the page was cut: Justify set a new line height, and every new look reworked the margins. Now Justify only spreads the words across each line, the margins change only when he changes them, and the page he was reading stays the page he is reading.
