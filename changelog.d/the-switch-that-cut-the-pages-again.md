---
title: The Switch That Cut the Book Into New Pages
try: In Pride and Prejudice, open Your Page, then Fine-Tune, and flip Justify on and off.
---
A third of the way into Pride and Prejudice, a reader flipped Justify and the page jumped back, his sentence now halfway down a page he had already read. The switch had been quietly changing the line height too, so the whole book was cut into new pages. Justify now only spreads the words across each line, and the page he was reading stays the page he is reading.
