---
title: The Deck That Would Not Leave
try: Stand the Duo half open like a tiny laptop, read a page, then tap the page twice.
---
Half open on a table, the Duo used to slam a solid panel across the bottom half of the screen: the chapter, the arrows, the slider and every button, standing there forever like a guest who has missed every hint. The page stopped dead at the fold, sometimes halfway through a line. Now the page fills the whole screen, as it does when the Duo lies flat, and the controls are one pane of glass floating over the lower half. Tap the page and they come; tap again and they go.
