title: The Words That Lost Their Ends
try: On the Duo folded, open Your Page, then Fine-Tune, and look at Line Spacing, Margins and the fonts.
---
On the narrow Duo, Fine-Tune lopped the ends off its own choices, offering a reader "Sn…", "Cla…" and "Ro…" as if he should guess, and sliced the last font clean in half at the panel's inner edge. Now every choice keeps its whole name, and the fonts glide from one edge of the panel to the other.
