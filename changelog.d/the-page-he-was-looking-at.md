---
title: The Page He Was Looking At, Kept Even Mid-Turn
try: Turn the iPad while a page is still settling, then leave at once and reopen the book.
---
He turned the iPad and, while the page was still settling into its new layout, left the book. The reader saved where it believed the page was rather than where it was on screen, and that could be a page early or even the start of the chapter. Reopening took him somewhere he had not been reading. Now the reader looks at what is on screen and keeps that page, so the book reopens where his eyes were.
