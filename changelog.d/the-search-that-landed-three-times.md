---
title: The Search That Landed Three Times
try: Search a long book for a name from its first chapters and tap a result from far away.
---
A reader searched for a name and tapped the result, and the screen could not make up its mind: two pages drawn on top of each other, then a page that was neither here nor there, then at last the right one. Three pages for one tap. The search now finds the match's page while the old page still waits, dimmed, and lifts straight onto it, once.
