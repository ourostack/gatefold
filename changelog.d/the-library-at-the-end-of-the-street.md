title: The Library at the End of the Street
kind: feature
try: In the Add menu, tap Browse Catalogs, open Project Gutenberg, search for Austen and tap Get. Then tap Read.
---
Gatefold once read only what one cloud allowed it. Now it can go out and fetch. Browse Catalogs in the Add menu opens Project Gutenberg and Standard Ebooks, thousands of free books with nothing to set up. Search, tap Get, and the book walks onto your shelf marked From a catalog, with Read waiting beside it. Keep your own books on a calibre or Calibre-Web server, Kavita or Komga? Add its address, and a user name and password if it asks, and browse it the same way. Passwords stay in your Keychain, and a book you already have never arrives twice.
