---
title: The Reopen That Fell to the Chapter Start
try: On an iPad, read into a chapter, rotate it, leave without turning, then reopen.
---
He came back to his book, and as the iPad laid the pages out again the reopen slid all the way back to the first page of his chapter, with nothing to bring it back. Now, if the new layout leaves him off his saved place before he has touched or turned anything, the book puts him back on it once, and then leaves the page to him.
