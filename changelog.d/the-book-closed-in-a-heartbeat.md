---
title: The Book Closed in a Heartbeat
kind: polish
try: Open a book and close it again at once, before its first page appears.
---
A reader opened Pride and Prejudice, thought better of it, and closed it in the same breath. The page had not even been drawn yet, so as the reader slid away it carried an empty hole where the page should be. Now a book closed that fast is drawn for one last look, and its page leaves with it.
