---
title: The Page He Never Saw
try: On an iPad on its side, open a book again and leave the app before its cover lifts, then open it once more.
---
A slow reopen keeps its cover up until his own page is ready. Behind that cover, a page still finding its place could be marked as the one he was reading, and leaving then saved it in place of his own. Now nothing behind the cover counts as seen, and leaving before it lifts keeps the page he saved.
