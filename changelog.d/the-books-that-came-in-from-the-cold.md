---
title: The Books That Came In from the Cold
kind: feature
try: In Files or Mail, share an EPUB and pick Gatefold. Then share the same one again. On iPad, drag a book onto the shelf.
---
For too long a book handed to Gatefold from another app knocked, was let in, and then stood in the hall unseen. No more. Open an EPUB in Gatefold from Files, Mail or Safari and it walks straight onto your shelf, with Read waiting in the note that says so. Drop books on the shelf on iPad. Pick a whole folder in Files and every book inside comes along. The same book twice stays one book, and a book already in your PocketBook Cloud takes the file instead of growing a twin. Books Apple Books keeps as folders come aboard too, and the new Apple Books guide in the Add menu says honestly what can follow you over.
