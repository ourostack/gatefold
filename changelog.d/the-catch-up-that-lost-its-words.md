---
title: The Catch-Up That Lost Its Words
try: On an iPad, turn it as a book opens, tap the catch-up offer at once, then leave and reopen.
---
The iPad was still turning its pages to the new shape when he tapped the catch-up offer, and the page from his other device swapped in at once. But the turn it replaced never finished, so the book kept only a rough guess of the page, not his words, and the reopen came back somewhere else. Now the swap ends that unfinished turn, the landing keeps his words, and the reopen shows the page he caught up to.
