---
title: The Key That Stopped Turning
try: Stay signed in for a few days, then open a book that lives only in your Cloud.
---
PocketBook Cloud sometimes refuses to renew Gatefold's sign-in. When it did, every download failed and the shelf asked you to sign in again, even though nothing about your account had changed.
Now Gatefold keeps your password in this phone's Keychain, never synced and never logged, and quietly signs in again on its own when that happens. You're asked only if the Cloud turns the password down, for example after you change it, and then Gatefold forgets the old one so it never uses up your login attempts. Finding your account by email also tries harder, because the Cloud sometimes can't find it on the first few tries.
