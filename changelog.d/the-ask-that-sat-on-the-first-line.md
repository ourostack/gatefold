---
title: The Notice That Sat on the First Line
try: Let your PocketBook sign-in lapse while reading, then turn a few pages.
---
The Cloud wanted a fresh sign-in, and its notice planted itself over the top of the page, hiding the first line of every page that followed. Readers turned and turned and never met the opening words. The ask now waits in the top margin as a small glass Sign in tab, where the chapter title usually sits, and one tap opens sign-in. Bring up the controls to read the whole notice or choose Later.
