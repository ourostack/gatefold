---
title: The Ledger Owns Up
kind: polish
---
Gatefold has quietly kept a tally of downloads and syncs that go wrong, so we can mend them. It never noted a title, a name or a word you read, but the privacy page still swore it kept nothing at all. Now the app carries Apple's privacy ledger declaring exactly what goes out, and the page says the same, line by line. Every copy of Gatefold now signs its reports with the same name, so none carries an ID of your phone.
