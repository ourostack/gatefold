---
title: The Veil That Would Not Lift
try: Jump to a far chapter, and while it is still loading, swipe or turn the page.
---
A jump to a chapter that would not load used to hold a pale veil over the screen until it gave up, and the reader could do nothing but wait.
Now the veil knows its limit. It lets go, and the chapter shows its own loading while the jump carries on. If he turns, swipes or jumps somewhere else meanwhile, the late jump bows out quietly. It writes no place over his page and offers no way back to a landing he never saw.
