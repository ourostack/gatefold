---
title: The Tap That Went Unheard
try: Tap a book that lives only in your Cloud, then tap it again while it's still coming down.
---
A tap on a Cloud book that never set off a download (because one was already under way, or because the book had no Cloud copy to fetch) left no trace, so a stuck download looked exactly like no tap at all.
Now every tap on a book that isn't on your phone yet leaves a small note: what the shelf showed, whether the Cloud has a copy, and, for a download already running, roughly how long it has been at it. Never the book's name, and never yours.
