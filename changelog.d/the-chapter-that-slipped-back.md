---
title: The Chapter That Slipped Back to Its First Page
try: In Pride and Prejudice, open Contents, tap a chapter late in the book, wait, then leave and reopen it.
---
He chose a chapter from Contents and the page went there, but while that chapter was still loading the book quietly saved the first page of its file instead. Close and reopen, and he was flung pages back, far from where he had chosen. Now a Contents jump keeps the chapter he tapped, even when it loads slowly, and if he turns or taps while it is on its way, the jump gives way to him.
