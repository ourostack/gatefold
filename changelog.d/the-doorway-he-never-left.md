He dragged the scrubber deep into a chapter not yet opened. The reader opened the chapter's first page, the doorway, and meant to walk on to his place. On a slow device it never did. He was left at the doorway, and nothing remembered it: his page number, his saved place and Back all stayed on the page he had left. Now, if the jump comes back still standing at the doorway, the reader walks him to his place again and keeps where he lands.
