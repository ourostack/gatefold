---
title: The Slip Between the Pages
kind: fix
try: Open a book while PocketBook Cloud wants you to sign in again, and look at the top of the page.
---
A reader turned page after page, never knowing the Cloud had quietly stopped listening. Only the shelf knew, and the shelf was a whole book away. When he picked up his e-reader, it had no idea where he had been. Now, the moment the Cloud wants you to sign in again, even on opening a book, a slip appears at the top of the page: one tap on Sign In, your email already filled in, and the place you read goes straight over. Later puts it away until you open the book again.
