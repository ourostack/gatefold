---
title: Held Like a Book
try: On the Duo, open a book flat, then fold it half open without turning it, the way you'd hold a paperback. Open it flat again.
---
Folding the open Duo half open, spine upright, used to treat it as if it stood on a table: one line of text ran straight across the hinge on the top half, and the controls took the bottom half, leaving about six lines to read. Opening it flat again could leave it that way. Now half open with the spine upright is a book: a page on each side of the fold, the same spread as flat, so opening and closing it moves nothing. Turned on its side and stood on a table, it is still the tabletop, with the page above the fold and the controls below.
