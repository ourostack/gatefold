---
title: The Pages He Turned on His Way Out
try: Turn two or three pages, go Home at once, swipe Gatefold away, then open it again.
---
He turned three pages, flicked Home before the last one had settled, and the phone, needing the room, quietly ended Gatefold. The place it saved was from a page or two earlier, still waiting on its way to the shelf, and the book reopened behind him. Now the page under his last turn is written the moment he leaves, with its first words when they have been read, so the book reopens on the page he left.
