---
title: The Clock That Would Not Stay Gone
kind: fix
try: On the open Duo held upright, tap the page to bring up the controls and wait. Then scroll on the closed Duo.
---
On the open Duo, upright, the controls brought a curse. The clock vanished, then returned, then vanished again,
several times a second. Close leapt back and forth across the corner and the page reflowed under every leap.
Now the clock is hidden the first time and stays hidden, and Close stays put. On the closed Duo, scrolling text
had been held below the camera with the chapter's name sitting on its first line. The text now runs to the top
of the screen and nothing covers it.
