title: The Door That Never Said Where He Was
try: On a slow device, drag the scrubber far ahead into a chapter not yet opened, wait for the page, and look at the page number.
---
He dragged the scrubber deep into the book and landed mid-chapter, with the words in front of him. But on the way in, the reader had only heard about the chapter's first page, the doorway, and it waited to hear about the page he actually reached. That news never came. His page number, his saved place and Back all stayed on the page he had left. Now, once the jump is back and his page is on screen, that page is kept.
