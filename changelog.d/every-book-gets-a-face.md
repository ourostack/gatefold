---
title: Every Book Gets a Face
kind: fix
try: In the Add menu, choose Browse Catalogs, open a catalog and look down the list of books.
---
Catalog lists had a ghost problem. Where a book's cover should have been, an empty grey square stared back, and a catalog behind a password showed nothing but grey. Now each row shows the catalog's own picture of the book, fetched with that catalog's sign-in so a password-protected library shows its covers too. When the catalog has no picture, or it's still on its way, the book wears a cover made from its title and author, in the same cloth as the books on your shelf. No more blank squares.
