---
title: The Chapter Start That Stole His Place
try: Turn a few pages, leave at once, then open the book again on a slow device.
---
He turned three pages, left in a hurry, and came back to find himself at the top of the chapter. Behind the opening cover, a page still parked on the chapter's first lines had been taken for his place, and the cover gave up moving him back. Now nothing under the cover is taken as his place until his own page is back, and the reopen lands where he left.
