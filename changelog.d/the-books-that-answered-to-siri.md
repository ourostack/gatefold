---
title: The Books That Answered to Siri
kind: feature
try: In Shortcuts, add List Books, Open Book or Sync Reading Place, and run it.
---
Siri and Shortcuts could not reach the library at all. Now they can list the shelf, open a book by its title, and send the reading place on, and each answers in a sentence. A book still on the cloud is refused with a reason, and a book on screen must be closed before another opens.
