---
title: The Taps That Went Nowhere
try: Open a book from the shelf, swipe a few pages on and one back, then tap the middle of the page and its edge.
---
A reader swiped from the cover into the first chapter, then tapped the page to bring up the controls. Nothing. Tapped the edge to turn. Nothing. Not a flicker, until the iPad was turned on its side and back. The swipe had begun on a page that was swapped away before it could say the finger had lifted, and the reader went on waiting for that finger, ignoring every tap that came after it. Now any finger that touches the glass on its own clears the wait, and every tap gets its answer.
