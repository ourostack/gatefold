---
title: The Page That Split in Two
kind: fix
try: Read on the Duo's cover screen or an iPad held upright, and tap the top of the page now and then.
---
Our reviewers watched in horror as a page on the Duo's folded cover screen, and on an iPad standing upright, tore down the middle into two cramped columns of four words a line, and stayed torn through every fold, scrub and trip to Contents. The culprit was a hidden switch, meant only for our own tests, that a tap at the top of the page could hit. The switch now hides unless a test asks for it, and the reader refuses to set two pages side by side on any screen too narrow or too tall to hold them.
