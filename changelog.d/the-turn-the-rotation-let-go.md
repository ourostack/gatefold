---
title: The Turn The Rotation Let Go
try: On an iPad, tap to turn the page and turn the iPad at once, mid-chapter and on a chapter's last page.
---
A reader on an iPad tapped to turn the page and turned the iPad before the page had landed. If the page he turned to had not yet answered, as when the next chapter was still loading, the reader let his turn go: he was back on the page he had turned from, or left on a page half laid out. Now the page he turns to is read before the turn moves, and a turn into a new chapter keeps that chapter's first page, so the turn survives the rotation.
