title: Upright Again, On His Page
try: On an iPad, jump to a chapter from Contents, turn the iPad on its side, then turn it back upright.
---
Turned back upright, the page stood for a moment in the spread's two columns before it became one. If the reader's place was checked in that moment, it found his words in the left column, so it let the page be. Then the page became one column, a page before his. Now the place is checked only once the page is laid out in one column, and he is back on his own page.
