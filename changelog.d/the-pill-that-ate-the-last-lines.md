---
title: The Pill That Ate the Last Lines
try: Open a book you were reading on another device, or jump somewhere from Contents, and read to the foot of the page.
---
After a jump, the little glass Back pill used to lay a slab of paper across the foot of the page and stand on it, and the last lines of the page simply vanished beneath. Readers finished chapters without their final sentences and never knew. The pill now floats over the page like the glass it is: the words run on under it and show around it, and in scroll mode the last line can scroll up clear of it.
