---
title: The Leaf That Froze Mid-Air
try: On the Duo, stand the book half open, then open it flat, in scroll mode and with pages.
---
Opening the Duo flat from half open used to raise a grey page from the spine, as if the book were being unfolded from its cover, and there it stood, frozen in mid-air over a blank white page, a monument to a page turn nobody asked for. In scroll mode, where pages don't even turn, it stood all the same. Now bending the Duo between half open and flat simply lays the page out again where you are; only opening the book from its cover, or shutting it, still turns a leaf.
