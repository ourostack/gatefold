---
title: The Leaf That Froze Mid-Air
try: On the Duo, stand the book half open, then open it flat, in scroll mode and with pages. Then turn the open Duo on its side and back.
---
Opening the Duo flat from half open used to raise a grey page from the spine, as if the book were being unfolded from its cover, and there it stood, frozen in mid-air over a blank white page. Turning the open Duo on its side shut a leaf and swung one open again, over a page cut off at the edge. Now bending or turning the Duo simply lays the page out again where you are, the old page held whole until the new one is ready; only opening the book from its cover, or shutting it, still turns a leaf.
