---
title: The Words That Slid Down the Page
try: In a book, open Your Page or Fine-Tune and change the font, text size, line spacing or margins, then change it back.
---
Halfway through Pride and Prejudice, one tap on a bigger text size or a new font and the line he was reading sank up to a hundred words down the page, or more, and he had to hunt for it. Now, on one page, a new font, size, line spacing or margins starts the page with the words he was reading, and changing back brings the same page back.
