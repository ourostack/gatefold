---
title: The Brush That Moved His Place
try: On an iPad in portrait with page curl, brush the middle of a page sideways with a fingertip, quickly, and let go.
---
A brush too short to turn a page turned nothing, as it should, except on a busy iPad. There the page underneath could slip in while nobody looked, and the words he had not reached yet were saved as where he was. The next open would have skipped him ahead. Now a brush that has already let go moves nothing at all, however late the iPad gets to it.
