---
title: The Buttons That Stood on the Words
kind: polish
try: On the Duo folded, open a book and tap the middle of the page to bring up the controls.
---
On the Duo's small outer screen, a reader called up the controls and found the Contents, Share and type
buttons planted right on top of the sentence they were reading, the words running out from under them on
both sides. Now the page's own paper rises behind those buttons, ending in the gap above the first line
they would touch, so every control stands on blank paper and no line is ever cut in half.
