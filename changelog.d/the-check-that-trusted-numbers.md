---
title: The Check That Trusted Numbers
kind: polish
try: Nothing changes on screen; this is the reader checking itself after you turn or unfold the device.
---
After every turn of the iPad or fold of the Duo, the reader checks that the words he was reading are still on screen. When it could not read the words on the page, it guessed from page numbers instead, and those can point at the page before. A check that guessed could call a lost page fine. Now it passes only when it finds his words, and when it could not look, it says so instead of guessing.
