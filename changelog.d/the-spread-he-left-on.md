---
title: The Spread He Left On
kind: fix
try: Turn an iPad on its side while a page is still settling, leave at once, and come back: you return to the spread you left.
---
Turn an iPad on its side and leave while the new spread is still settling, and coming back could put you half a spread off. You landed on the page you were reading upright, not the spread you'd been looking at. While the page settled, the reader still had the upright page as your place, and it never checked the screen to see what you were actually looking at. Now, as you leave, the reader reads your place from the screen, so you come back to the spread you saw.
