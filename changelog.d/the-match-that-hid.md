title: The Match That Hid
try: On the Duo folded, open Search, look for "Darcy" and tap a result.
---
A reader on the narrow Duo searched for a name and got back a column of sentences that ran out before ever reaching it, the bold word lost somewhere past the third line. He tapped one in hope and arrived on a page with nothing to show where the name had gone. Now each result begins a few words before its match, so the bold name is right there, and the page he lands on underlines it.
