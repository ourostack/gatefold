---
title: The Contents That Clung On Past the Top
kind: fix
try: Open Contents, scroll a little way up the list, then pull it down past its top and let go.
---
Contents opens on the chapter you're reading, part way down its list. Pull it down from there and the list scrolled back to its top, stretched, and sprang back, and Contents stayed up however far you pulled, on iPad and on the Duo's cover screen alike. Only the close button or a pull from the very top let it go. Now a pull that carries the list past its top closes Contents when you let go, as Apple Books does, and you're still on the page you left. Flicking up and down the list to find a chapter still never closes it.
