---
title: The Page That Forgot Its Words
try: On an iPad, turn a page, rotate the iPad and leave before the page settles, then reopen.
---
He turned a page, then turned the iPad, and while the new layout was still being worked out he left. The book had saved his exact words a moment before, yet on the way out it wrote over them with a bare page number, and the reopen landed a page early. Now leaving on the same page he saved keeps his words, so the reopen comes back to the page he was reading.
