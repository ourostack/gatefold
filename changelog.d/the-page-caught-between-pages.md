---
title: The Page Caught Between Pages
kind: fix
try: On an iPad, turn it on its side and back upright while a page is still settling, and check the page opens on the words you were reading.
---
He turned the iPad on its side, then upright again, and the reader looked for his words while the page was still sliding into place. Caught halfway between two pages, it saw his words, decided all was well, and let the page come to rest one page too early. Now the reader waits for the page to stand still on one page before it looks, and if his words aren't there, it takes him back to them.
