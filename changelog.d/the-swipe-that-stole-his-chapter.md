---
title: The Swipe That Stole His Chapter
try: In Scroll, read partway into a chapter, swipe sideways and tap the clock at the top; then scroll past the chapter's end and back.
---
Deep in a chapter in Scroll, one stray sideways brush flung him into the next, and swiping back dropped him at that chapter's start or end, never his line. A tap on the clock at the top hurled him to the chapter's first page. Both tricks are gone: in Scroll a sideways swipe does nothing and a tap at the top leaves the page where it is. Chapters now turn when he scrolls on past the end, and scrolling back past the next one's start returns him to where he left off.
