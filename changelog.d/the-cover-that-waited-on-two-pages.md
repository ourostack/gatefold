---
title: The Cover That Waited on Two Pages
try: With another device further on in a book, open it on this one for the first time.
---
A book opened for the first time, with the Cloud further ahead, sat behind its cover far longer than it should, because two pages were loading at once: the first page, and the page further on that Catch up would land on. On a slow device, neither won. Now the first page loads alone, the cover lifts, and only then does the page further on start loading behind it, ready for the tap.
