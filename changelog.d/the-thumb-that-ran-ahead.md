---
title: The Thumb That Ran Ahead
try: Search for a name, tap a result far from your page, and watch the scrubber as the page arrives.
---
Jump to a far-off match and, for a moment, the old page lingers while the new one is fetched. The scrubber's thumb used to dash ahead in that moment, standing at the destination over a page that hadn't moved yet, so the reader couldn't tell which one to trust. Now the thumb waits with the page and moves to the new place as soon as it appears.
