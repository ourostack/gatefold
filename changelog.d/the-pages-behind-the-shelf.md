---
title: The Pages Behind the Shelf
try: Turn on VoiceOver, wait on the shelf a moment, and swipe through it.
---
To open a book the instant it is tapped, Gatefold lays its page out quietly behind the shelf, and counts its pages there too. VoiceOver could find those hidden pages: swipe far enough along the shelf and it would start reading a book nobody had opened, and every look the system took at the shelf had to wade through them all. Now they wait out of reach until their book is tapped, and the shelf holds only the shelf.
