---
title: The Note That Knew Its Place
kind: polish
try: In the Add menu, choose Choose from Files and pick two books. Watch the top of the shelf as they arrive.
---
When books came aboard, a cheerful note announced them by sitting right on top of the shelf's big Library title and the Add button, as if it owned the place. Now the note takes its own spot under the title. The title stays readable, Add and your account stay within reach, and the shelf scrolls back to the top so you see the news. After a few seconds the note folds away and the covers slide back up. When one book came in, its Read button waits until you use it or close the note.
