---
title: The Ribbon That Read Too Soon
try: Tap a book on the shelf the instant it appears, on a page you bookmarked, and watch the ribbon.
---
Tap a book fast enough and the reader read its page before the page had finished arriving, then trusted that early glimpse for as long as you stayed. The bookmark ribbon could miss a mark that was right there on the page. Now the reader looks again the moment the page comes up, so the ribbon matches the words in front of you.
