title: The Circles Who Snuck Back In
try: In a book, open Your Page, tap Fine-Tune and look at the Back button; on the shelf, look at the Add button.
---
Under cover of night, plain circles and stiff rounded corners crept back into Gatefold: onto the Back button in Fine-Tune, the shelf's Add button, the close button on panels, Fine-Tune's pills and the grey ghosts of a loading shelf. A squircle patrol has rounded up every one of them, and each is a soft squircle again. A watchman now stands at the gate and turns away any circle that tries it again. Only the honest round things stay: the slider's thumb, your avatar and the little dot that wants your attention.
