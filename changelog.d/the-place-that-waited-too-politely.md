---
title: The Place That Waited Too Politely
try: Read with the phone offline for a minute, go back online, close the book and lock the phone, then open the e-reader.
---
The signal dropped mid-chapter, and the reading place, rebuffed once, sat down to wait its turn. Then the reader closed the book and put the phone away, and the place kept waiting, politely, in a phone that had gone to sleep. The e-reader never heard of it. Now, when the book closes or Gatefold leaves the screen, a waiting place goes at once instead of sitting out its wait.
