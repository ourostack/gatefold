---
title: Pick One, Read One
kind: polish
try: In the Add menu, choose Choose from Files and pick a single EPUB. Then pick two at once.
---
Pick one book in Files, or send one to Gatefold from Mail, Safari or any app, and it doesn't stop at the shelf any more: it opens, ready to read. Send one while you're reading something else and the new book takes its place; the one you were reading keeps your page on the shelf. Pick a handful at once, or a whole folder, and they settle onto the shelf together, waiting for you to choose.
