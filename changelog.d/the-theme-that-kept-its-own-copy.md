---
title: The Theme That Kept Its Own Copy
kind: polish
try: Browse the shelf and open a book in light and dark, and look at the buttons and the bookmark.
---
Gatefold once carried its own hand-copied list of all 44 house colours, and every time the house repainted, that copy fell a little further out of date, like a map of a city that keeps moving its streets.
The copy is gone. Gatefold now takes the house palette straight from Ouro UI 0.8.0 and ties only its ribbon blue in, so every new coat of paint reaches the shelf the day it dries.
