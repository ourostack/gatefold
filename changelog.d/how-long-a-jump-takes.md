---
title: How Long a Jump Takes
try: Jump far with the scrubber or a search, and open a book from the shelf.
---
Jumping far in a book or opening one can leave the old page dimmed for seconds. Gatefold now notes, in bucketed counts and with nothing about the book, how long each jump and each first page took to show, so the wait on a real iPhone or iPad can be measured and fixed.
