---
title: The Page That Stood Its Ground
kind: fix
try: On a folded iPhone Duo or an iPad, tap the middle of a page to bring up the controls, and watch the words.
---
One tap for the controls, and the page flinched: it stepped back, and blank paper swept up from the foot until half the words were gone. No more. The page now holds perfectly still, every line where it was. Close and Bookmark float at the top, beside the camera, and the scrubber, Contents, Share, Your Page and Search float in one row along the foot, just above the home bar, in glass.
