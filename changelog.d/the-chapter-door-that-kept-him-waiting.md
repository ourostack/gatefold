---
title: The Chapter Door That Kept Him Waiting
try: On a slow device, drag the scrubber far ahead into a chapter not yet opened, leave at once, and reopen.
---
He dragged the scrubber deep into the book and landed mid-chapter. But the chapter had loaded slowly, and on the way in its first page had flashed by. That page was taken for his place, and the real one, arriving a beat later, was turned away. He reopened the book at the chapter's door. Now the first page on the way in is only a doorway, and the place he lands on is the place kept.
