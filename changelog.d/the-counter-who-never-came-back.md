Behind every page, six quiet counters go through the book chapter by chapter so the numbers can be shown. Sometimes one would wander into a chapter and never come back, and the whole party waited for it forever. The numbers never returned, and "Counting pages" stayed put.
Now the others wait only so long. Then a fresh counter takes up the lost chapter, the count finishes, and the numbers come home.
