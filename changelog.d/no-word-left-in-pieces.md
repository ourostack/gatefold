---
title: No Word Left in Pieces
kind: polish
try: Add a book with no cover picture by a long-named author, such as Mary Wollstonecraft Shelley, and look at it on the shelf.
---
Books without a cover picture get one made from their title and author, and on a phone's narrow shelf covers the long words used to fall apart: WOLLSTONECRA on one line, FT on the next, and Franken- hyphenated over stein. Now a made cover keeps every word whole. A line shrinks a little until its longest word fits, and a word too long even then ends neatly in an ellipsis instead of spilling onto the next line. Author names now sit centred under the title too.
