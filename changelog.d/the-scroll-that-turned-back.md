---
title: The Scroll That Turned Back Time
try: In scroll mode on an iPad or iPhone, turn it on its side and back, flick down a screen or so, and turn it again while the page is still gliding.
---
You flicked down past the next chapter heading, turned the device to read on its side, and found yourself back where you had been a screen and a half earlier, as if the scroll had never happened. The reader had not yet heard where your flick came to rest, so it put you back at the last place it knew. Now a turn stops the page where it is gliding, reads the words at the top of your screen there, and keeps them at the top through the turn.
