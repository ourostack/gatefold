---
title: The Slip That Could Not Make Up Its Mind
try: Read ahead on your e-reader, open the same book here, and compare the slip's page count with its folded tab.
---
Your e-reader ran ahead, and the slip that tells you so had a confession: the number of pages it said depended on how fast the app happened to count. The same place came out as 106 pages one time and 91 the next, and the folded tab could disagree with the slip it came from.
Now the slip starts from its best guess and takes the counted number as soon as the count reaches that page, so once it has, the slip and its tab give the same number.
