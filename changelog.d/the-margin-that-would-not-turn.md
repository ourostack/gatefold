---
title: The Margin That Would Not Turn
try: Open a book on an iPad on its side and tap near either outer edge, just inside the words.
---
On the iPad's two-page spread, a reader tapped the outer edge of the page to turn it, and the controls rose up instead. Again. And again. Nine taps, nine sets of controls, and not one page turned. The spread's turn zone had shrunk to a sliver at the very edge of the paper. Now each page's outer quarter turns it, on the iPad and on the unfolded Duo, and a tap on the middle of a page still brings up the controls.
