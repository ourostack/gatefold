---
title: The Jump That Was Never Saved
try: In Pride and Prejudice, drag the scrubber far into the book, then leave at once and reopen it.
---
He dragged the scrubber to a new place and left before that chapter had finished loading. Nothing was saved, so the book came back at its very start, with no sign of where he had gone. Now a jump he makes is kept as his place even while its page is still on its way, and once the page arrives, its own first words take over.
