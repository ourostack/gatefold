Gatefold

Privacy, in one breath.

Your books, your place in them and your notes travel only between your device and your own PocketBook Cloud account. The one thing Gatefold sends us is a few anonymous counts about how downloads, syncs and page loads went, so we can fix what goes wrong.

Last updated 27 November 2026. Applies to Gatefold Reader for iPhone and iPad.

Who Gatefold talks to

PocketBook Cloud (cloud.pocketbook.digital), on your behalf: to sign you in, list your books, download the ones you open, fetch their covers from the links PocketBook Cloud provides, and sync your reading position, highlights, notes and bookmarks. There is no Gatefold server. We never see your books, your account, your email or what you read.

PostHog (us.i.posthog.com), for the diagnostic counts below. Nothing else.

The diagnostic counts

The app sends a short report at four moments:

  • When it downloads a book: which step it reached, the status or error code it got, how many tries it took, the file format, and yes-or-no facts such as whether the book was already on the device.
  • When it syncs your place: whether the send or fetch worked, the status or error code, what started it, and roughly how long ago you turned the page.
  • When you tap a book that isn't downloaded yet: that a tap happened, the state the book was in, and roughly how long it had been downloading.
  • When a book opens or you jump far ahead: roughly how long the page took to show.

Each report also says which version of Gatefold and of iOS sent it, and the time. Times are rounded into wide steps, such as "under 5 seconds" or "under 2 minutes".

  • Never a title, author, file name, link, highlight, note, email, password or error message.
  • Every copy of Gatefold sends the same fixed name in place of an ID, so no report can be told apart from another phone's. Nothing in a report identifies your device or your PocketBook account.
  • PostHog is told not to look up where you are from your IP address and not to build a profile.

This is what App Store Connect lists under App Privacy as diagnostics, product interaction and performance data, all "not linked to you" and none used for tracking. The full list is in GatefoldTelemetry.swift.

Your sign-in

When you sign in, your email and password go straight to PocketBook Cloud, which hands back a session token. Gatefold keeps that token, and your password so it can sign you back in when the token runs out, in your device's Keychain, on this device only (neither is synced to iCloud). Neither ever leaves the device except to PocketBook Cloud. Signing out removes them and the cached shelf.

One small Keychain note stays behind on purpose: for each book, the last reading position Gatefold sent to PocketBook Cloud, so Gatefold can recognise its own bookmark when the Cloud hands it back, even after a reinstall.

What stays on your device

Downloaded books, their covers, your highlights and notes, your reading settings and the EPUB files you open from the Files app live in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

What Gatefold doesn't do

  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and no crash reporting service.
  • No accounts of our own, and no data sold, shared or used to profile you.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

PocketBook Cloud's side

What PocketBook Cloud does with your account and library is covered by PocketBook's own privacy policy, since it's their service. Gatefold is an independent app and isn't part of it.

Children

Gatefold isn't aimed at children. The diagnostic counts are the same for everyone and say nothing about who is reading.

Changes and questions
