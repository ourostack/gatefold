Gatefold

Pick up on the very same line.

Gatefold is a calm little reader for your PocketBook Cloud library, on iPhone, iPad and every posture of iPhone Duo.

Sign in with your PocketBook Cloud account and your shelf turns up, covers and all. Open a book and you land on the exact line your e-reader left off, with a soft underline so your eyes know where to go. Put the phone down, pick the e-reader back up, and your place has already gone on ahead.

What it does

  • Syncs your reading position both ways, down to the line.
  • Carries highlights, notes and bookmarks between your e-reader and your phone.
  • Opens like a real gatefold on iPhone Duo: folded, flat as a two-page spread, or half-open on the table.
  • Six page colours, from Daylight to Midnight, and it can follow the sun.

What it costs

Nothing, forever. No ads, no subscriptions, no tracking. It's the reader we wanted, so we made it and gave it away.
